**EN:** A 44-step tutorial quest built into the addon.

**TH:** เควสสอนเล่น 44 ขั้นที่ฝังอยู่ในแอดออน


**EN:** A per-player step-by-step teaching quest that follows the current addon: **44 steps in 8 chapters**, no rewards. Steps are detected automatically (items, menus, actions). Open it from **Script Tool › Guides & Items** or **Status › Progression**. Notifications and Thai language can be switched in Settings.

**TH:** เควสสอนเล่นทีละขั้นของแต่ละผู้เล่นตามแอดออนปัจจุบัน: **44 ขั้น 8 บท** ไม่มีรางวัล ตรวจจับขั้นอัตโนมัติ (ไอเทม เมนู การกระทำ) เปิดจาก **Script Tool › Guides & Items** หรือ **Status › Progression** ตั้งค่าการแจ้งเตือนและภาษาไทยได้ใน Settings


## Chapter 1 · Basics / พื้นฐาน

| Step | Title / หัวข้อ | Do / ทำอย่างไร |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Get a Stats Book<br><i>รับ Stats Book</i> | Craft a Stats Book with 4 Sticks. It opens your Status Menu: stats, Prestige, Mastery and Ascension. (The Script Tool, from an Admin, opens every menu in one place.) |
| 1.2 | Open the Status Menu<br><i>เปิด Status Menu</i> | Right-click the Stats Book to open the Status Menu. Everything about your character starts here. |
| 1.3 | Reach Level 1<br><i>ถึงเลเวล 1</i> | Earn XP: kill mobs (Zombie 5, Creeper 8, Blaze 15), mine ores (Coal 1, Diamond 10), eat food or go fishing. Level 1 needs 50 XP. |
| 1.4 | Spend a Stat Point<br><i>ใช้ Stat Point</i> | Every level gives 4 Points. Status Menu > Character & Gear > Stats Manager > Add Stats. Weapons, armor and tools need a minimum stat (see the item's Lore), or they will not work. |
| 1.5 | Read the Knowledge Book<br><i>อ่าน Knowledge Book</i> | Craft a Knowledge Book (1 Dirt) and open it. It explains every system in English and Thai. |

## Chapter 2 · Prestige / Prestige

| Step | Title / หัวข้อ | Do / ทำอย่างไร |
|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Reach Level 40<br><i>ถึงเลเวล 40</i> | Level 40 is the cap at Prestige 0. You can Prestige when you are at your maximum level. |
| 2.2 | Craft 2 Prestige Sigils<br><i>คราฟต์ Prestige Sigil 2 ชิ้น</i> | Resonant Ink x2 + Diamond Block + Gold Block. Your first Prestige costs 2 Sigils; later ones cost more. |
| 2.3 | Prestige for the first time<br><i>Prestige ครั้งแรก</i> | Status Menu > Progression > Prestige. Resets level, XP, Points and stats; keeps all items, Perks, Mastery and unlocks. You gain +20 max level and a higher stat cap. Augment unlocks at Prestige 1. |
| 2.4 | Pick a Perk<br><i>เลือก Perk</i> | Each Prestige gives you one Perk to choose (Scholar, Plunderer, Vigor ...). Open the Prestige menu and pick one. |

## Chapter 3 · Augment / Augment

| Step | Title / หัวข้อ | Do / ทำอย่างไร |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Craft a Reforge Hammer<br><i>คราฟต์ Reforge Hammer</i> | Craft a Reforge Hammer: 2 Iron Blocks + 3 Dark Oak Logs. It is used on an anvil for every Reforge action. |
| 3.2 | View Total Statistics<br><i>ดู Total Statistics</i> | Status Menu > Character & Gear > Total Statistics. It lists the Augment bonuses on your gear and lets you switch single stats on or off. |
| 3.3 | Reroll an Augment<br><i>Reroll Augment</i> | Side task. Hold your Hammer and right-click an anvil (or Shift + right-click): Reroll costs hammer durability + 1 Augment Crystal. Enchanted mobs drop 1-5 Crystals; 1 Crystal + Diamond + Lapis crafts 2 Crystals. |
| 3.4 | Lock an Augment stat<br><i>Lock ค่า Augment</i> | Side task. At the anvil choose Lock to protect a good stat from future Rerolls (hammer durability + 2 Augment Crystals). |

## Chapter 4 · Chorus / Chorus

| Step | Title / หัวข้อ | Do / ทำอย่างไร |
|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | Reach Prestige 2<br><i>ถึง Prestige 2</i> | Chorus unlocks at Prestige 2. Climb back to your max level (now 60) and Prestige again. |
| 4.2 | Craft the Ultimate Enchanter<br><i>คราฟต์ Ultimate Enchanter</i> | Uncarved Gem x4 + Stone Bricks + Crafting Table + Enchanting Table. Uncarved Gems drop from Wither, Warden and Ender Dragon (Ascension 2+), Geode mobs (Ascension 4+) and Awakened mobs (Ascension 7+). Ask your Admin if you have none yet. |
| 4.3 | Brew Resonant Ink<br><i>ปรุง Resonant Ink</i> | Brewing Stand: Dragon's Breath + Echo Shard makes Resonant Ink, the currency of Chorus, Runes and the Prestige / Ascension items. |
| 4.4 | Roll an Echo element<br><i>สุ่มธาตุ Echo</i> | Ultimate Enchanter > Chorus > Roll, level Echo (6 Resonant Ink). Each armor piece has 2 element slots. Wear the armor. |
| 4.5 | Activate a 2-piece set<br><i>ปลดเซ็ต 2 ช่อง</i> | Wear armor whose slots hold the SAME element in 2 slots. 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 matching slots unlock stronger set effects. Lock a good element (Ink) and roll the rest. |

## Chapter 5 · Mastery & Ascension / Mastery และ Ascension

| Step | Title / หัวข้อ | Do / ทำอย่างไร |
|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | Open Mastery<br><i>เปิด Mastery</i> | Status Menu > Progression > Mastery (unlocked by Prestige 1). 11 permanent skill lines that Prestige never resets. |
| 5.2 | Level up a Mastery line<br><i>เลเวลอัพสาย Mastery</i> | Mastery lines level from what you do: fight (Melee, Ranged, Defense), mine (Gathering), travel (Travel), fish (Fishing), use Mana (Magic). Get any line to Level 1. |
| 5.3 | Craft an Ascension Relic<br><i>คราฟต์ Ascension Relic</i> | Resonant Ink x2 + Nether Star + Netherite Ingot. Ascension raises the difficulty of the whole world. |
| 5.4 | World reaches Ascension 1<br><i>โลกถึง Ascension 1</i> | Status Menu > Progression > Ascension. It affects every player and cannot be lowered. Mobs get tougher, but XP and loot improve. Anyone with Prestige 1 can do it. |
| 5.5 | World reaches Ascension 2<br><i>โลกถึง Ascension 2</i> | Ascension 2 needs a player with Prestige 2 and Ascension Relics. Bosses begin dropping Uncarved Gems at this level. **This does not unlock the Gem system by itself**: system access requires both Prestige 4 and Ascension 2. The world must reach this level; any eligible player can do it. |

## Chapter 6 · Gem / Gem

| Step | Title / หัวข้อ | Do / ทำอย่างไร |
|---|---|---|
| 6.1 | Reach Prestige 4<br><i>ถึง Prestige 4</i> | Gems need Prestige 4 + Ascension 2. Keep prestiging: each Prestige raises the max level by 20 and costs more Sigils. |
| 6.2 | Find an Uncarved Gem<br><i>หา Uncarved Gem</i> | Gems need Prestige 4 + Ascension 2. Uncarved Gems drop from Wither, Warden and Ender Dragon (Ascension 2+), Geode mobs (Ascension 4+) and Awakened mobs (Ascension 7+). 9 Gem Dust also craft one. |
| 6.3 | Craft an Engraver<br><i>คราฟต์ Engraver</i> | Amethyst Shard + Netherite Ingot + Amethyst Block. It carves gems and engraves sockets, and has limited durability. |
| 6.4 | Carve a Gem<br><i>แกะ Gem</i> | Hold an Uncarved Gem in your main hand and the Engraver in your off hand, then right-click. The gem tier is random (1% chance of 2 gems). |
| 6.5 | Engrave a Socket<br><i>เจาะ Socket</i> | Ultimate Enchanter > Engrave Socket, pick an armor piece. Each armor piece has one socket; success chance is 33%. Hold the Engraver in your inventory. |
| 6.6 | Insert a Gem<br><i>ใส่ Gem ลงช่อง</i> | Ultimate Enchanter > Insert Gem. The gem tier needs enough Mag: 15 / 30 / 45 / 60 / 75 / 90 / 115 from Common to Mythical. |
| 6.7 | Enchant a Gem ability<br><i>Enchant Ability ให้ Gem</i> | Ultimate Enchanter > Gem Enchanting: choose the ability of a gem (Gem Dust x12 per tier). Each tier has 12 abilities for helmet, chest, legs and boots. |
| 6.8 | Fuse Gems<br><i>รวม Gem (Fusion)</i> | Anvil with a Hammer > Gem Fusion: 5 gems of one tier become 1 gem of the next tier (or 2 gems + 1 Augment Core). |

## Chapter 7 · Rune / Rune

| Step | Title / หัวข้อ | Do / ทำอย่างไร |
|---|---|---|
| 7.1 | Reach Prestige 6<br><i>ถึง Prestige 6</i> | Runes need Prestige 6 and Ascension 3. Keep prestiging: each Prestige raises the max level by 20 and costs more Sigils. |
| 7.2 | World reaches Ascension 3<br><i>โลกถึง Ascension 3</i> | Raise the world to Ascension 3 (needs a Prestige 3 player and Ascension Relics). Mobs get much tougher; the Sculk tier appears. **This alone does not unlock Runes**: Rune access requires Prestige 6 + Ascension 3. |
| 7.3 | Craft an Uncarved Rune<br><i>คราฟต์ Uncarved Rune</i> | Legend Gem + Amethyst Shard (shapeless). |
| 7.4 | Carve a Rune item<br><i>แกะไอเทมรูน</i> | Hold the Uncarved Rune with the Engraver in your off hand and use it (Shift = x16). You get a Rune item of a random grade: Gamma < Delta < Theta < Sigma < Phi < Psi < Omega (T1 to T7) with a random Rune inside. |
| 7.5 | Insert a Rune<br><i>ติดตั้ง Rune</i> | Anvil > Rune > Insert: puts the Rune item into the weapon or tool in your main hand (weapons and tools only; armor uses Gems). Use Recarved or Reroll with Resonant Ink to change it. |
| 7.6 | Trigger a Rune<br><i>ให้ Rune ทำงาน</i> | Fight or mine with the Rune equipped until it triggers. Active Runes (Stormcall, Warden's Echo): Sneak + use the item. Matching Chorus elements raise a Rune's level. |

## Chapter 8 · Advanced Chorus / Chorus ขั้นสูง

| Step | Title / หัวข้อ | Do / ทำอย่างไร |
|---|---|---|
| 8.1 | Roll a Resonance element<br><i>สุ่มธาตุ Resonance</i> | Chorus > Roll, level Resonance (16 Resonant Ink): Lightning, Frost, Gale, Metal, Light, Dark. Wear the armor. |
| 8.2 | Roll a Transcend element<br><i>สุ่มธาตุ Transcend</i> | Chorus > Roll, level Transcend (30 Resonant Ink + 1 Stellar Core): Divine, Demon, Life, Death, Star, Void, Dimension, Time. Stellar Cores drop from Core mobs. Wear the armor. |
| 8.3 | Wear a Hybrid set<br><i>สวมชุดผสม (Hybrid)</i> | Armor with exactly 2 different elements of the same level forms a Hybrid set (Fire + Water = Steam). Slots = 2 x the smaller count. Needs Hybrid Mixing on in the Chorus menu. |
| 8.4 | Complete an 8-slot set<br><i>ครบชุด 8 ช่อง</i> | Fill all 8 slots (4 armor pieces) with one element or one Hybrid pair to unlock its strongest effect. |
| 8.5 | Fuse an Awaken element<br><i>Fusion ธาตุ Awaken</i> | Chorus > Fusion: 2 Transcend slots of a pair (Divine + Demon = Eclipse, Life + Death = Samsara, Star + Void = Cosmos, Time + Dimension = Eternity) on one piece. Needs Chorus Mastery 15, Prestige 3 and 3 Stellar Cores. Permanent. |
| 8.6 | Fuse a Melodious element<br><i>Fusion ธาตุ Melodious</i> | Two Awaken pieces make Destiny (Eclipse + Samsara) or Infinity (Cosmos + Eternity). Needs Chorus Mastery 20, Prestige 6 and 6 Stellar Cores. |
| 8.7 | Complete the Genesis set<br><i>ครบชุด Genesis</i> | Fuse Destiny + Infinity into Genesis (Chorus Mastery 25, Prestige 10, 12 Stellar Cores) and wear the full 8-slot Genesis set. The final step of the Guide Quest. |

## Following the Guide Quest in order · วิธีทำ Guide Quest ตามลำดับ

**EN**
- Start at **Chapter 1 · Basics** and follow the numbered step IDs in ascending order (for example, 1.1, 1.2, 1.3). Continue chapter by chapter through the listed 44 steps across 8 chapters.
- Read the **Do** column for the action that triggers each step. The quest detects supported actions automatically; opening a menu or obtaining an item may be a required action, not merely a suggestion.
- Reopen Guide Quest from **Script Tool › Guides & Items** or **Status › Progression** to check the current step if you are unsure what comes next.
- If a step does not advance, verify that you performed the listed action with the required item/menu and then check the quest's current step. Do not skip ahead based on chapter titles alone.
- The quest has no rewards; its purpose is to guide players through the addon's systems.

**TH**
- เริ่มจาก **Chapter 1 · Basics** แล้วทำตามหมายเลขขั้นจากน้อยไปมาก (เช่น 1.1, 1.2, 1.3) ไล่ไปทีละบทจนครบ 44 ขั้นใน 8 บท
- อ่านคอลัมน์ **Do** เพื่อดูการกระทำที่ใช้ตรวจจับขั้น เควสจะตรวจจับการกระทำที่รองรับโดยอัตโนมัติ การเปิดเมนูหรือรับไอเทมอาจเป็นเงื่อนไขที่ต้องทำจริง ไม่ใช่เพียงคำแนะนำ
- เปิด Guide Quest อีกครั้งจาก **Script Tool › Guides & Items** หรือ **Status › Progression** เพื่อตรวจสอบขั้นปัจจุบันหากไม่แน่ใจว่าต้องทำอะไรต่อ
- หากขั้นไม่เลื่อน ให้ตรวจว่าทำตามการกระทำที่ระบุโดยใช้ไอเทมหรือเมนูที่ถูกต้องแล้วหรือยัง จากนั้นตรวจขั้นปัจจุบัน อย่าข้ามขั้นจากการดูชื่อบทอย่างเดียว
- เควสไม่มีรางวัล จุดประสงค์คือแนะนำระบบต่าง ๆ ของแอดออน
